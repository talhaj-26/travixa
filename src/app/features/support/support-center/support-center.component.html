KNOWLEDGE BASE

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers for the most common TRAVIXA traveller support scenarios.
How can I check my booking status?

Open My Bookings from your account to review confirmation, payment status, booking type and journey details.

What happens if my payment fails?

Check the payment section first. If the transaction remains unresolved, create a support case with your booking reference.

Can I cancel a booking?

Cancellation depends on the booking type and applicable booking policy. Open the relevant booking to review available actions.

Where can I find my booking reference?

Your booking reference is displayed in My Bookings and Booking History after a reservation is created.

How do I update my profile?

Visit User Profile to review your account information, preferences and security settings.

How can I get help with transport?

Visit Transportation for available services or open a support case under the Transport category.
