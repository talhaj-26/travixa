🧳 Booking & Reservations

How do I make a booking on TRAVIXA?
Select a service such as a hotel, tour package, restaurant or transport option, review its details, choose your required date and guest information, then continue through checkout.

Start a booking →
How do I know whether my booking is confirmed?
A successful booking receives a booking reference and a confirmation status. You can review the full reservation from your booking details or My Bookings area.

Check my bookings →
Can I change my booking after confirmation?
Modification depends on the service's availability and booking policy. Open the booking details and check whether modification is available for that reservation.

💳 Payment & Refunds

What payment methods can I use?
Available payment methods are shown during checkout. The checkout page provides the selected payment option, booking amount and final payable total before confirmation.

Open checkout →
When will I receive a refund?
Refund timing depends on the cancellation policy, service provider and payment method. If a refund is approved, the transaction status should be reflected in your payment or booking records.
What should I do if my payment fails?
First verify your payment details and try again. If the issue continues, keep the transaction information and contact TRAVIXA support for assistance.
